THE KIRA CASE

THE BEGINNING

One notebook.
One god.
One new world.

Light Yagami was an exceptionally intelligent Japanese high-school student who discovered a mysterious notebook known as the Death Note.

After discovering its power, Light adopted the name Kira and began eliminating criminals, believing that he could create a world without crime.

His vision gradually transformed into an obsession with becoming the god of a new world.
