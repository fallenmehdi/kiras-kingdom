THE KIRA CASE

THE BEGINNING

One notebook.
One god.
One new world.

Light Yagami was an exceptionally intelligent Japanese high-school student who discovered a mysterious notebook known as the Death Note.

After discovering its power, Light adopted the name Kira and began eliminating criminals, believing that he could create a world without crime.

His vision gradually transformed into an obsession with becoming the god of a new world.

DEATH NOTE RULES

THE BOOK OF DEATH

Write the name.
Control the fate.

The Death Note is a supernatural notebook capable of killing any human whose name is written inside it.

RULE 01

The Name

The human whose name is written in the Death Note shall die.

RULE 02

The Face

The writer must have the person's face in mind when writing their name.

RULE 03

The Cause

If the cause of death is written within 40 seconds, it can determine how the person dies.

RULE 04

The Time

Details surrounding the death can be written shortly after the cause of death.

RULE 05

Limitations

The Death Note has strict rules. Not every imagined action or circumstance can be carried out.

RULE 06

The Shinigami

Every Death Note is connected to a Shinigami, a supernatural being associated with death.
